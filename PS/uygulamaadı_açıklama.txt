Kısa açıklama: Offline, encrypted vault for secure notes, passwords, and personal data.

Tam açıklama: Lynra GreyNote is a private, offline vault designed to securely store your most sensitive information.

🔒 Private by Design
Your data never leaves your device. Lynra GreyNote does not use servers, does not track you, and does not collect any personal data.

🛡 Strong Encryption
All records are protected with modern encryption. Only you can access your data using your PIN or biometric authentication.

📱 Fully Offline
No internet connection is required. Your notes, passwords, and personal information are always available and always under your control.

📂 Secure Storage
Store passwords, private notes, and sensitive information in one safe place with a clean and minimal interface.

⚡ Fast and Simple
Designed for speed and simplicity. No unnecessary features, no distractions — just secure and reliable storage.

🔐 Your Responsibility
For maximum security, your credentials are not recoverable. If you forget your PIN or pattern, your data cannot be restored.

Lynra GreyNote is built for users who value privacy, simplicity, and full control over their data.
