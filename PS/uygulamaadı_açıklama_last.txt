Tam Açıklama (Full Description)

Lynra GreyNote is a private, offline vault designed to securely store your most sensitive information. If you are looking for an encrypted notes app that respects your data privacy, this is your safe haven.

🔒 Private by Design & Secure Notepad
Your data never leaves your device. Lynra GreyNote does not use servers, does not track you, and does not collect any personal data. It is a strictly private notes application.
No Ads, No Subscriptions

🛡 Strong Encryption
All records are protected with modern encryption. Only you can access your encrypted notes using your PIN or biometric authentication.

📱 Fully Offline Notes
No internet connection is required. Your offline notebook, passwords, and personal information are always available and always under your total control.

📂 Secure Storage & Password Vault
Store passwords, private notes, and sensitive information in one safe place with a clean, minimal, and premium interface.

⚡ Fast and Simple
Designed for speed and simplicity. No unnecessary features, no bloated code, no distractions — just secure and reliable storage.

🔐 Your Responsibility (Zero-Knowledge)
For maximum security, your credentials are not recoverable. If you forget your PIN or pattern, your encrypted data cannot be restored.

Lynra GreyNote is built for users who value privacy, simplicity, and full control over their digital life.
